Questions

Is it a printed book?

No — it’s a digital book. We don’t print or ship books. You read and listen to it online on any phone, tablet or computer and download it as a PDF. If you’d like a paper copy, the book includes print-shop files and a print specification: take them to any local print shop.

Can I change something after I see the book?

Yes. Just reply to our email with what you’d like to change — a name, a detail in the story, an illustration. Corrections are free.

Is there an audiobook?

Yes, every book comes with an audiobook read by a professional narrator. It plays right in the online book, and the QR code in the printed copy opens it too.

How long does it take?

Usually 3–5 days after payment. We will tell you the exact date by email.

Will my child really look like themselves?

Yes — every illustration is drawn from your photos, in a 3D cartoon or watercolor style. The clearer the face in the photo, the better the likeness.

What happens to the photos?

They are used only to draw your child's book and are deleted once the book is ready. We never publish them.

How do I pay?

After you email us, we reply with the price and payment instructions (Zelle, US). We start your book as soon as the payment arrives. If you have a gift certificate, the book is free — just send us its number.
