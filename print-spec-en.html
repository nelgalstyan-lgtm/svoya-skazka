Geroenok

Print Specification

Personalized children's book by Geroenok · digital printing, run of 1+ copies.

For the customer: give the print shop this document and the two files from the “For the print shop” button in your book (pages and cover). The “Download PDF” file is not suitable for professional printing — it has no bleed.

1. Product

Trim size“Big Story” — 154 × 235 mm (6.06 × 9.25 in)
“Fairy Tale” — 210 × 297 mm (A4)
ColorsInterior 4/4, cover 4/0
Page countAs in the file: always an even number, page 1 is a right-hand page

2. Files

InteriorPDF, all pages in order.
Page size with bleed: 160 × 241 mm (“Big Story”) / 216 × 303 mm (“Fairy Tale”) — 3 mm bleed on every side.
CoverOne-sheet PDF: back + spine + front (front on the right). 5 mm bleed on every side. The spine width is in the file name.
ImageRaster, 300 dpi, each page is a single image (fonts are already rasterized — no outlining needed). No spot colors, varnish or special finishes.
ColorRGB (sRGB). Convert to CMYK with your standard press profile, no color correction.

3. Safe zones

  • Text and page numbers are at least 14 mm from the trim line.
  • The thin decorative frame of the “Big Story” is 6 mm from the trim: trimming tolerance ±1 mm max.
  • The interior is imposed for binding: on right-hand pages the decoration is at the outer edge and the text is shifted toward the spine. Gutter margin is at least 14 mm.
  • QR codes (last page and back cover) — do not scale down, print at 100%.

4. Materials and binding (recommended)

Interior paperMatte coated 130–150 gsm (full-page illustrations, no show-through). The coloring pages at the end work with pencils and markers on this paper.
SoftcoverCoated 250–300 gsm, matte lamination 1/0.
Binding“Big Story” — perfect binding.
“Fairy Tale” — saddle stitch (2 staples) or perfect binding.
Hardcover (case binding)Possible. The cover file has 5 mm bleed; board wrap needs 15–20 mm — extend the background at the edges (the edge background is plain or textured) to your template.

5. Spine

The spine width depends on the paper and page count. Tell the customer the exact width: they will enter it in the “Spine width” field and download the cover again — it takes a minute.

Guide: page count ÷ 2 × sheet thickness (matte coated 130 gsm ≈ 0.1 mm) + 1 mm for the cover and glue.

6. Pre-press check

  1. Page count and order in the interior file match the book; page 1 is a right-hand page.
  2. Cover: front on the right, spine width matches your calculation.
  3. Trim to the trim size; bleed is cut off completely.
